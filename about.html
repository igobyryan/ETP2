Writer

Gerry has written more than 100 research publications, spanning ecosystem function, natural resource management, and the development of human ecology as a field of study. He is the author of two books, both available as free downloads: Traditional Agriculture in Southeast Asia: A Human Ecology Perspective and Human Ecology: Basic Concepts for Sustainable Development. The latter has been translated into multiple languages and provides the complex adaptive systems framework this toolkit draws on for understanding how communities and ecosystems interact.
